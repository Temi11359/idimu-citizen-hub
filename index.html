About the LCDA

Isheri-Olofin Local Council Development Area (LCDA) is dedicated to bringing governance closer to the people. We focus on enhancing community development, infrastructural growth, education, public health, and socioeconomic empowerment for all residents.

Our mission is to foster a safe, sustainable, and prosperous environment through participatory governance and progressive public administration.

Available Services

Application and processing of state/local government origin certificates.

Requirements

  • Valid means of identification
  • Proof of residency (utility bill or tenancy agreement)
  • 2 recent passport photographs

Fee: ₦5,000

How to Apply: Visit the Administration Department at the LCDA Secretariat with the requirements listed above during office hours.

Licensing and registration for micro, small, and medium scale enterprises.

Requirements

  • CAC registration (where applicable)
  • Proof of business address
  • Valid means of identification

Fee: Varies by business category

How to Apply: Submit your application and documents online via the Citizen Assistant, or in person at the Trade & Commerce desk.

Official legal marriage registration services at the council registry.

Requirements

  • Valid means of identification for both parties
  • 2 witnesses aged 21 or above
  • 2 recent passport photographs per party

Fee: ₦10,000

How to Apply: Book an appointment at the council registry at least 21 days before the intended date.

Access to primary healthcare centres, immunizations, and maternal health care.

Requirements

  • None for general consultation
  • Immunization card for follow-up visits

Fee: Free / Subsidized

How to Apply: Walk in to the nearest Primary Health Centre during clinic hours — no appointment required.
